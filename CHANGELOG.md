## 1.0.0

- Initial release of `bouncy_press`.
- Tactile spring press and bounce physics for any widget.
- Features:
  - `BouncyPress` widget and `.bouncy(...)` extension on `Widget`.
  - Configurable `shrinkScale`, `dimOpacity`, `pressDuration`, and `releaseDuration`.
  - Configurable `pressCurve` and `releaseCurve` (spring-like ease-out curve).
  - Built-in haptic feedback options (`light`, `medium`, `heavy`, `selection`, `none`).
  - Graceful scroll cancellation and multi-gesture handling.
  - Zero external dependencies.
